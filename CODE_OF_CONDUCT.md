# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code, docs, behavior, and tradeoffs.
- Avoid harassment, intimidation, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Respect privacy and never post secrets, private account details, or personal data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate these standards. Maintainers may also limit or block participation when needed to protect contributors and users.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through GitHub repository maintainers. For security vulnerabilities, use `SECURITY.md` instead of public issues.
